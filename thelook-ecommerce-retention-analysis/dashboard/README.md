# TheLook E-commerce Growth & Retention Analysis - Dashboard

his dashboard is the final delivery layer of the TheLook E-commerce Growth & Retention Analysis project. It is designed to support recovery efforts after the post-COVID business impact, and provides a concise view of key performance indicators, helping stakeholders track profit fluctuations, identify fast-growing or high-return product segments, and monitor customer retention trends across time.


---

## Purpose & Design Framework

Built with a stakeholder-first approach and aligned with core dashboard design principles:

| Step | Focus             | Details                                                                 |
|------|-------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **1. Who** | Target Users      | Marketing teams, business stakeholders     |
| **2. Why** | Purpose           | Track growth and retention KPIs to identify opportunities and risks     |
| **3. What**| Metrics Displayed | Profitability, Order Behavior, Customer Value, Returns, Geography       |
| **4. How** | Visualization     | KPI cards, trend lines, sales tables, maps, time-based breakdowns      |
| **5. When**| Usage Frequency   | Monthly performance reviews, campaign assessments, executive reporting  |

---

## KPIs and Charts Included

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Gross Sales** – Total sales value for selected date range  
- **Net Profit** – Profit after deducting costs  
- **Average Order Value (AOV)** – Revenue per order  
- **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)** – Revenue expected per customer over lifetime  
- **Customer Retention Rate** – % of returning customers  
- **Order Return Rate** – % of orders returned

Each KPI includes percentage and absolute change vs. previous period for trend analysis.

---

### Visual Charts and Tables

- **Monthly Sales Comparison**  
  - Line chart comparing revenue across 2021–2024  
  - Tracks seasonal growth or decline

- **Product Performance Table**  
  - Top-performing SKUs by item price, total sales, gross sales  
  - Returns column for monitoring high-return products

- **Sales by Hour of the Day**  
  - Line chart showing total sales volume per hour  
  - Helps identify peak selling hours and optimize marketing timing

- **Revenue Geographical Breakdown**  
  - Interactive world map visualizing revenue concentration by country/region  
  - Aids in geo-targeted campaign strategy
